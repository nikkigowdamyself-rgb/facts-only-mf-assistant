# Sample Q&A

## 1. What is the minimum SIP for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?
**Answer:** The minimum SIP shown on the official HDFC ELSS Tax Saver page is ₹500.  
**Source:** HDFC ELSS Tax Saver official scheme page: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct

## 2. What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?
**Answer:** The lock-in period is 3 years from the date of allotment of the respective units.  
**Source:** HDFC ELSS Tax Saver official presentation: https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/Others/2025-10/HDFC%20ELSS%20Tax%20saver%20Presentation%20%28October%202025%29.pdf

## 3. What is the exit load for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
**Answer:** An exit load of 1.00% applies if units are redeemed/switched out within 1 year from allotment; no exit load applies after 1 year.  
**Source:** HDFC Flexi Cap Fund official scheme page: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct

## 4. What is the minimum SIP for HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund?
**Answer:** The official scheme page shows a minimum SIP of ₹100.  
**Source:** HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund official scheme page: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund/direct

## 5. What is the risk level of HDFC Large Cap Fund?
**Answer:** The official HDFC scheme page labels the scheme risk as Very High.  
**Source:** HDFC Large Cap Fund official scheme page: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund/regular

## 6. What is the benchmark for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
**Answer:** The official HDFC page lists the NIFTY 500 Total Returns Index as the benchmark.  
**Source:** HDFC Flexi Cap Fund official scheme page: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/regular

## 7. How can I get a consolidated account statement?
**Answer:** HDFC Mutual Fund provides a Download Statement service, and AMFI also explains e-CAS through CAMS/KFintech and CAS through MF Central.  
**Source:** AMFI Download CAS: https://www.amfiindia.com/online-center/download-cas

## 8. Should I invest in HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
**Answer:** I can provide facts about the scheme, but I can’t recommend buying, selling, switching or choosing a fund. You can ask for its minimum SIP, exit load, riskometer, benchmark or official documents.  
**Source:** AMFI Investor Corner: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor
